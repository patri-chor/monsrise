# T019 Decision - Rework Required

## Decision

Rejected for rework, not because the tree optimization results are invalid, but because the delivered implementation did not satisfy the complete resource contract of T019.

## Accepted Evidence

- All 24 authoritative T017 frozen candidates were processed.
- Candidate-level tree optimization used the canonical eight-opponent panel and recorded 16 effective candidate workers with peak 16 on 16 logical CPUs.
- The quality result is evidence-bearing: 5 tree-optimized candidates, 17 deck-only, 2 archive, and 0 quality-gate qualifiers; dominant diagnosis is optimizer no-op / no valid split or IG.
- No deployment, active-library mutation, or historical artifact overwrite occurred.

## Blocking Defect

The independent final baseline/final evaluation phase in `src/engine/tree/sequential_tree_optimization.ts` iterates candidates in a serial loop and calls `evaluateFormationOnPanel` directly. T019 required candidate-level parallel final evaluation under the same 16-worker cap and persisted actual evaluation concurrency evidence. The report records 16 workers for optimization, but not for final evaluation.

## Rework

T020 must reuse the completed optimization results and rerun only the independent final evaluation and quality decision with bounded candidate-level parallelism. It must not repeat the 862.5-second tree optimization workload.
